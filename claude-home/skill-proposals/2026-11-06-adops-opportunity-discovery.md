# Skill Proposal: adops-opportunity-discovery
Date: 2026-11-06
Source: Gabriel's ad-ops interview and Powers retrospective conversation; GitHub #590, #646 and #647.

## Trigger

A trader/ad-ops user needs to describe current workflows, friction and desired tools so engineering can assess product opportunities and a business case. Use after the agreed product delivery/handoff when that is a user constraint.

## Draft Steps

1. Check the agreed interview timing; gather only relevant available context and label its provenance. Map current processes and existing automation with at most two questions per turn.
2. Capture desired tools/results in user terms. Distinguish user-owned workflow changes from missing data/access/capability; deepen two or three opportunities with normal and exception cases and per-step human-control boundaries.
3. Capture baseline active effort, review/rework, volume and business value. Use incremental weekly economics for steady work and per-wave economics for episodic peaks; leave engineering estimates unknown.
4. Give the full notes and evidence to a separate reviewer for contradiction, assumption, authority and arithmetic checks. Label fallback self-audit and unresolved facts honestly.
5. Produce a self-contained opportunity brief and live-call agenda. Preserve source/estimate/hypothesis distinctions and report the result on the existing business issue without changing implementation scope or contacting participants implicitly.

## Boundaries

This is product discovery, not employee performance evaluation or staffing approval.
No software design, code, live campaign action, invented ROI or promised partner access.
Do not assume Claude has all prior conversations or that scheduling fixes missing capability.
If this becomes an active skill, use skill-creator and validate it on an actual completed interview before claiming effectiveness.
