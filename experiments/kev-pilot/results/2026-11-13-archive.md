## What remains unproven

- Better recall, task completion, cost, or safety than the current harness has not been established.
- Authored diagnostics are not the planned independent set of at least 100 queries. Blind regrading of authored cases does not change their sampling origin.
- The existing metadata candidate selector and a body-reading reranker do not provide an adequate full-text control. A missing essential page in the candidate pool cannot be recovered by reranking that pool.
- Sparse relevance judgments leave unjudged pages unknown. The retained blind review contains unresolved disagreements. No headline effectiveness score is justified.
- Hosted Jev claims about latency, pricing, calibration, and security do not transfer automatically to this local Kev runtime.

## Reviewed harness-health plan

The [plan](https://github.com/gabriel-amyot/claude-dev-settings/blob/bca3929/docs/plans/harness-health/PLAN.md), [investigation](https://github.com/gabriel-amyot/claude-dev-settings/blob/bca3929/docs/plans/harness-health/INVESTIGATION.md), and [adversarial review](https://github.com/gabriel-amyot/claude-dev-settings/blob/bca3929/docs/plans/harness-health/REVIEW.md) are retained at their reviewed revision. Plan readiness is not operational qualification.

The plan reuses the existing repeatable evals and observatory, but first qualifies their evidence: suite registration, failure propagation, source and host fingerprints, actual policy assertions, and coverage. Its inventory is dated 2026-10-31 and must be refreshed before implementation.

Four accepted review requirements remain in force:

1. Prevent writes and outbound traffic before tests execute. A post-run filesystem comparison is insufficient isolation.
2. Admit known, authorized project scope before reading session history. Unknown scope is denied.
3. Compare the actual operational baseline, a full-text lexical control, and Kev over the same controlled pool as separate arms.
4. Establish independent essential-document references before making general recall claims.

The proposed health view keeps safety, quality, efficiency, human effort, and coverage/freshness visible separately. Unknown evidence remains unknown. Critical safety failures veto a favorable summary. No overall numeric score, cross-host parity, passive collection, or scheduled monitoring was qualified in this work.
