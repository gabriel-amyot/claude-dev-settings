# Skill Proposal: non-expert-interview-depth-probe
Date: 2026-06-11
Source: Data Engineer candidate evaluation (Gab interviewing for a domain he's not expert in)

## Trigger
Evaluating or interviewing a candidate (or assessing any engineer's claim) in a domain where the interviewer is NOT the expert, and you need to tell real depth from fluent bluff. Also: comparing multiple candidates' interview transcripts.

## Scope
global (user-level)

## Draft Steps
1. For any technical claim, ask "why" up to 3 times — depth keeps getting more specific; bluff collapses into a buzzword or a loop.
2. Demand a story ("tell me a time…") over a concept; require a number, a failure, a name.
3. Flag passive voice / tool-deferral ("it's handled by X") as a depth-masking tell; probe past the tool's built-ins.
4. Require the tradeoff: "what did you give up?" — all-upside answers are suspect.
5. Score calibrated honesty (admitting an edge: "I used AI for this, no hands-on") as positive; score confident bluffing as negative.
6. Before ranking multiple candidates: enforce evidence parity (same questions asked, same transcript fidelity). Never infer missing content from a partial transcript — ask someone who was present.
7. On a saturated team, weight the scarce skill over the abundant one; prefer the candidate who complements the bench's gaps over one who duplicates its strengths.
