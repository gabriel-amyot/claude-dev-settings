# STE software-term allowlist
#
# Terms exempt from the linter's banned_word and nominalization checks. The
# ASD-STE100 approved dictionary is ~900 words and rejects legitimate software
# and adtech nouns, so the "-tion/-ment/-ance/-ence of" nominalization pattern
# and the banned-word list would false-positive on normal engineering prose.
#
# One term per line, lowercase, matched case-insensitively. A term listed here
# is NOT counted as a nominalization or banned word. Everything else still fires.
#
# Grow-path: when a legitimate software/adtech/Klever term trips a false
# positive during calibration or a real post, add it here in the correct
# section and commit. Keep it sorted within a section. Do NOT add hedges,
# marketing adjectives, or phrasal verbs here — those stay flagged by design.
